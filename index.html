1. Who we are

"Meow Inc.: Cat Tycoon" (the "Game") is a casual idle game for Android and web browsers. This policy explains what information is processed when you play. Contact: clogos.games@gmail.com.

2. Information the Game itself stores

The Game does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, location or any other personal information and has no accounts of its own. Your game progress (coins, businesses, settings) is saved:

  • on your device (Android app storage or your browser's local storage);
  • on web portals that offer cloud saves (Yandex Games, CrazyGames), in the portal's storage linked to your portal account, if you are signed in there;
  • on Android, if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, in your Google account (Play Games "Saved Games").

Progress data contains only game values and timestamps. We do not sell or share it.

Google Play Games (Android, optional). If you sign in, Google Play Games Services provides the Game with your Play Games player ID and display name (used only to show who is signed in) and stores your cloud save in your Google account. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in; you can delete the Game's Play Games data at any time in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account and data) or at myaccount.google.com.

3. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads (full-screen ads at natural breaks and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch). Ads are provided by third parties that may collect and process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device model, operating system, language and ad interactions, in order to show, measure and personalize ads and prevent fraud:

  • Android: Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses data from partner apps.
  • Yandex Games: Yandex Advertising Network — Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • CrazyGames — CrazyGames Privacy Policy.
  • Poki — Poki Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the Android version asks for your consent using Google's consent tool (UMP) before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy settings". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On web portals, consent is managed by the portal.

4. Children

The Game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear browser/site data. Cloud saves are kept by the portal under its own policy. To request deletion of any data related to you, write to the contact e-mail above.

6. Security

Data is transmitted only over encrypted connections (HTTPS) by the platforms and ad providers listed above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game means you accept the updated policy.

  • на вашем устройстве (хранилище приложения Android или локальное хранилище браузера);
  • на игровых площадках с облачными сохранениями (Яндекс Игры, CrazyGames) — в хранилище площадки, привязанном к вашему аккаунту на ней, если вы вошли в аккаунт;

Сохранение содержит только игровые значения и отметки времени. Мы не продаём и не передаём эти данные.
